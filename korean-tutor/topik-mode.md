# TOPIK mode

Activate this file only when the learner explicitly wants TOPIK placement, practice, or exam preparation. General Korean learning should use the level framework without turning every session into test preparation.

## Establish the exam and delivery

Confirm:

- TOPIK I, TOPIK II, or the separate TOPIK Speaking test;
- PBT or IBT;
- target date and required level/score;
- prior official scores by section;
- whether the need is certification, university, work, or self-assessment.

Formats and cut-offs can change. Before current advice, verify the NIIED/TOPIK official page listed in `sources.md`.

## What each result supports

- **TOPIK I:** listening and reading at beginner test level. It does not measure speaking or writing.
- **TOPIK II:** listening, writing, and reading at intermediate/advanced test level. Speaking is still separate.
- **TOPIK Speaking:** a separate score and level; do not merge it silently into TOPIK I/II.

Never describe an exam level as the learner's complete Korean ability without additional samples. A learner can reach a cut-off through strong reading while having much weaker interaction.

## Current checkpoints

As shown on the NIIED official information page (last modified 2025-09-17, checked 2026-09-28):

| Test | Sections (items) | Time | Total | Level bands |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| TOPIK I PBT | listening 30, reading 40 | 100 min | 200 | 1급 80-139, 2급 140-200 |
| TOPIK I IBT | listening 26, reading 26 | 70 min | 400 | 1급 121-235, 2급 236-400 |
| TOPIK II PBT | listening 50, reading 50, writing 4 | 180 min | 300 | 3급 120-149, 4급 150-189, 5급 190-229, 6급 230-300 |
| TOPIK II IBT | listening 30, reading 30, writing 3 | 125 min | 600 | 3급 191-290, 4급 291-360, 5급 361-430, 6급 431-600 |
| TOPIK Speaking | speaking 6 | 30 min | 200 | 1급 20-49, 2급 50-89, 3급 90-109, 4급 110-129, 5급 130-159, 6급 160-200 |

PBT and IBT scores are on different scales: never convert one into the other or quote a PBT cut-off for an IBT candidate. Recheck the official page before quoting any row to a learner; if it has changed, the page wins and this table must be updated.

## Baseline

Run a representative cold sample before building a plan:

- official released/sample material where permitted;
- real timing and interface constraints when the score matters;
- item-level answers, confidence, and evidence notes;
- writing saved exactly as submitted;
- no mid-section teaching.

A short placement set can locate a starting point but cannot predict a certificate precisely. Report the slice and uncertainty.

## Debrief by mechanism

Do not group only as listening/reading/writing. Useful mechanisms include:

- did not recognize a known word in connected speech;
- missed number, negation, correction, or speaker stance;
- vocabulary gap blocked the item;
- connective or sentence ending reversed the relation;
- referent or omitted subject was assigned incorrectly;
- chose real-world plausibility instead of text evidence;
- lost time on local detail before global structure;
- writing task content/organization failure;
- grammar, spacing, or register error in production;
- guessed from option pattern or test strategy with no Korean evidence.

For every miss, record the Korean evidence and the cheapest treatment. A right answer with a false rationale is a strategy warning, not mastery.

## Training versus mock exam

Training decomposes the skill and gives feedback between phases. A mock preserves the real order, timing, audio policy, navigation, and answer constraints. Do not call a set a mock if it omits major sections or permits pauses unavailable on test day.

Use official sample materials for fidelity. Generated material is acceptable for targeted drills, but label it and have a competent independent check of Korean naturalness, key validity, distractors, and task resemblance before using the score diagnostically.

## Writing preparation

Use the phased loop in `writing-loop.md`, then a cold timed task. Track separately:

- task completion and required content;
- organization and cohesion;
- vocabulary/collocation;
- grammar, morphology, spelling, and spacing;
- formal written register;
- independent versus hint-assisted performance.

Do not invent official scoring criteria. Use current public TOPIK guidance and released exemplars, and identify any tutor-created rubric as non-official.

## Forecasting and readiness

Use distributions or score ranges, not a single guaranteed level. Pool multiple cold samples. Report:

- total and section scores;
- source and form of each test;
- timing/fidelity deviations;
- confidence interval or honest range;
- probability or evidence for clearing the required cut-off;
- unmeasured skills.

Two cold full-format results at or above target are stronger booking evidence than one peak. The real official score outweighs tutor-made mocks and should recalibrate future estimates.

## Exam integrity

Do not obtain, reproduce, or coach from leaked live test content. Use official released samples, licensed preparation material the learner supplies within allowed use, and original practice items. Preserve copyright and test security.
